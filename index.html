Design that ships, not just designs that render.

I'm a UX engineer — I sit between product design and front-end implementation, which means I care as much about focus order and error states as I do about layout and type.

I like small teams where I can own a problem end to end, and I'm equally comfortable dropping into a solo contract to take a defined piece of work from spec to shipped.

This site is intentionally light on projects right now — it's built to grow. New work gets added here as it's finished, not staged for a big relaunch.
